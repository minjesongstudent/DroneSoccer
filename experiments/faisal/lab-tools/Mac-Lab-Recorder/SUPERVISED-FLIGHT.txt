EXPERIMENTAL PHYSICAL POLICY FLIGHT + RECORDING

THIS PACKAGE CAN START THE MOTORS AND FLY THE DRONE.
The policy is the exact seenflag-s1-level1, 85-input model. Its randomized
simulation development result was 48 clean repeats / 52 crashes out of 100.
This interface has software tests, not a completed physical flight validation.

1. With normal internet, run 1-Setup.command once and wait for all tests to pass.
2. At the lab, use the enclosed cage and fitted guards. Clear people from the
   flight enclosure. Keep an operator at the Mac and visually watching the drone.
3. Join the Tello Wi-Fi; close other drone controllers and recorders.
4. Run 2-Supervised-Flight.command and type FLIGHT. This connects the camera,
   starts recording and opens LOCAL controls. It does not automatically take off.
5. Verify live camera and telemetry. Check the supervision box. CLICK the green
   Take off + run policy button ONCE. No long press or holding is needed.
   It requests takeoff, then keeps running the policy until you stop it or a
   safety check requests landing. There is no fixed policy duration.
6. Press Escape / LAND NOW to request landing. Changing focus/tabs also
   requests landing. Keep the local flight page focused. Keep the page and
   Terminal open while the landing requests finish. Visually confirm the drone is down and motors stopped.

The separate red CUT MOTORS NOW control is an emergency motor cut: the drone
will DROP. LAND NOW / Escape requests landing, not a motor cut.
Do not control this flight through remote desktop; run it on the Mac next to
the drone. If Wi-Fi fails, software cannot guarantee a command reaches the drone.

DESIGN LIMITS (not measured safety guarantees)
  Fresh processed camera within .25 s, telemetry/browser heartbeat within .5 s.
  Policy inference watchdog .3 s. Takeoff acknowledgement/height wait 12 s.
  Preflight idle window 180 s; it does not limit an already-started flight.
  Policy/recording continue until operator or safety stop. Landing retries up to 20 s.
  Battery >=30%, temperature <80 C; ground height <=10 cm before start.
  Policy starts only after takeoff OK plus a new height reading >=30 cm.
  During policy control, reported height must stay 30..150 cm.
  No automatic restart or lateral position/geofence claim. One flight per launch.

Policy navigation commands are directly quantized using the existing team
mapping [50,50,100,70] for [right,forward,up,clockwise yaw]. Full normalized
policy values can reach these RC values. This is NOT a reduced-speed policy.
Vertical response and default camera intrinsics remain imperfectly calibrated;
all assumptions are recorded. Only real camera8 features and actually locally
sent command history enter the actor. Telemetry drives stop checks, not actor input.
Takeoff and landing are SDK/operator actions; navigation comes from the policy.

RECORDS: sessions/flight-<timestamp>/
  camera.h264, video_packets.jsonl: raw video, receipt times and byte offsets
  telemetry.jsonl: raw state packets with the SAME monotonic host clock
  commands.jsonl: takeoff/RC/land/emergency sends, responses and transport errors
  policy_events.jsonl: proposals before sending and send results
  policy_preview.jsonl: policy inputs, raw outputs, quantized sent actions,
                       frame times and inference times during policy flight
  preview_metadata.json: model hash, history lags, mapping and guard settings
  preview_outcome.json, session.json: errors and final software state
  comparison.csv / comparison-summary.json: timestamp-aligned telemetry/actions
  SHA256.json and exported ZIP: retained evidence and checksums

"Sent" means the UDP socket accepted the bytes, NOT that the drone received or
executed them. A landing request or low height reading is NOT motor-stop proof.
The logs contain measured telemetry, not an invented global XYZ trajectory.
Host receipt time is not exposure time or measured network delay.
