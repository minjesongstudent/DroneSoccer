  pass 3.5-5.9 s at level 1). But the evaluation was crash-heavy: seeds 0, 2, 3 crashed in 100% of episodes
  (0.33-0.64 passes/min, never left level 0), and seed 1 crashed in 62.5% (4.83 passes/min, level 0.75).
  Most crashes were net crashes (seed 2: net 72%, hit 28%, 0 timeouts). In training, seed 2 ran ~3.3
  passes/min with 14 s episodes and 98-100% crashed: about one pass, then into the net. The reward allowed
  this. A crash (-30) was only 10 worse than hovering forever (-R_TIME/(1-GAMMA) = -20). Pass then crash
  netted ~+1, plus whatever shaping the drone earned flying round before it hit the net. So "pass, fly
  round sloppily, die" was nearly as good as a careful lap, and much easier to learn.
  CHANGE: R_CRASH 30 -> 50. Nothing else changed (R_PASS 40, R_PROGRESS 4 with no GAMMA, R_TIME 0.1,
  R_WALL 1, GAMMA 0.995). The contract comments are updated to match.
  WHY: A crash now costs 30 more than hovering at the same spot, so dying is clearly worse than stopping
  or carrying on. Pass then crash nets ~-19, while pass then hover nets ~+11 and pass then a full lap nets
  far more. So the net is no longer an acceptable way to end. Hover is still -20 and crash -50, so
  crashing is never a cheap way out (R_TIME/(1-GAMMA) = 20 < R_CRASH). The shaping is still a pure
  difference of route distance, so any loop sums to zero and cannot be farmed. Risk: the higher penalty
  could make the approach look too risky and bring back hovering in front of the hoop. Progress already
  made is kept, though, and one pass is worth +31 net, so lining up should still beat parking once the
  pass is reliable. That is why the step is to 50 and not higher.
  EXPECT: Confirmed if the net share and crash rate fall well below 50% on most seeds, episodes get longer
  with 2+ passes, passes/min at level 1 rises above 1 on several seeds, and more than one seed levels up.
  Refuted if seeds go back to parking (timeouts up, passes near 0, first pass later than ~10 s), or if the
  crash rate and net share stay near 100% (then the net crash is about control and observation, e.g. the
  net not being visible to the policy, not about incentives).
