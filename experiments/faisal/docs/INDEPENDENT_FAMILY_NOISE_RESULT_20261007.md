# Single-action continuation targets remain noisy

The bounded collection completed 64 new state families and 1,536 unique learned-policy continuations. The declared whole-family split gives 52 fitting states and 12 held-out states; candidate actions and replicas never cross that split. The fitted actor was unchanged throughout.

For bootstrapped soft returns, comparisons between the first and last three replicas gave:

| Quantity | Fitting families | Held-out families |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pairwise action-preference sign agreement | 54.2% | 47.2% |
| Median absolute action-pair mean difference | 1.656 | 0.303 |
| Median paired standard error | 2.173 | 0.330 |
| Contrasts exceeding twice their standard error | 8.3% | 16.7% |
| RMSE between replica-half value means | 10.93 | 14.35 |

Finite-return sign agreement was 51.6% and 55.6%, respectively. For binary mission outcomes, most action comparisons were tied; only 32 fitting contrasts and four held-out contrasts were nonzero in both halves. No mission contrast exceeded twice its estimated standard error. Tied best actions are explicitly excluded from best-action agreement, so constant failure/success outcomes do not manufacture agreement.

The six contrasts within each state and trajectories within each family are dependent. These percentages are descriptive, not independent confidence intervals or adjusted significance tests. Six replicas remain noisy expected-value estimates. The results do not prove that actions have no effect; they show that this particular single-action/full-continuation target does not supply a stable ranking signal at the declared budget.

Do not train a critic or actor as though these estimated rankings were precise labels, and do not extend collection unchanged. Preserve the distinction between predicting the general value of a state and ranking small differences among its actions. A future diagnostic may compare longer learned decision sequences to address delayed credit assignment, but its movements must be generated by the learned policy at the actual observations. Replaying an open-loop command sequence into changed observations is not a substitute. Such an experiment is not implemented or authorized by evidence as a guaranteed fix.
