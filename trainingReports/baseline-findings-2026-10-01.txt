baseline-findings-2026-10-01
NOT A TRAINING RUN. Findings from the hand-written baseline (src/baseline.py Lapper),
in the simulator and on the real Tello, written to inform reward design.
written 2026-10-01   git 58b3057 (toms-branch)

SUMMARY FOR REWARD DESIGN
  1. Walls are the biggest sim-to-real gap. In the sim the geofence is soft: it clamps the
     drone 0.15 m from the net and charges R_WALL. The real net is hard: touching it tangles
     the props and ends the flight. 94% of sim baseline episodes touch the fence at least
     once; on the real drone that would be 94% crashes. Treat on_wall as close to a crash
     when shaping (a much larger R_WALL, or a penalty that grows with consecutive wall steps)
     and judge runs on the wall share, not just passes/min.
  2. Most wall contact comes from strafing sideways while lining up with the hoop (85% of
     first touches), not from the go-round. The policy cannot see walls, so the only defence
     is behaviour: short, decisive lateral moves, not long strafes.
  3. Approaching the hoop off its axis crashes. Flying straight at the centre from up to 25
     degrees off the hoop's axis clips the noodle (6.5 cm thick, 45 cm outer). Lining up
     first (ratio near 1) cut the baseline's crashes from 56% to 11%.
  4. The camera looks 12.8 degrees down. A hoop level with the drone shows at v = -0.58, not
     v = 0. Any shaping that rewards a centred v sends the drone ~23 cm high at 1 m, a whole
     hoop radius, into the rim. Never reward v -> 0.
  5. The hoop can be above the camera's view. From takeoff height (0.8 m) a hoop near 1.4 m
     is out of frame however the drone turns. Climbing during search raised the baseline
     from 0.65 to 0.82 passes/min in the sim, and it is how the real drone found the hoop.
  6. After a pass, getting back round to the front is the bottleneck. 86% of baseline
     episodes end in timeout, mostly because the blind go-round does not find the hoop again.
     Rewards that only pay for passes give little signal here; the _to_go progress term is
     what carries the agent round.
  7. Front-only passes need the marker. A hoop with no marker is either the back (a
     wrong_way crash) or not the hoop at all. Gating the approach on having seen the marker
     cost nothing in the sim and stopped the real drone chasing blue junk.

  The "base" line in reports written before this date used an older Lapper, and often
  an older sim and level, so it is not comparable with these numbers.

  Where the original crashed: 50-60% of episodes hit the rim right after losing sight of
  the hoop, before any pass. The drone was committed (forward > 0.5), lost the hoop in the
  last ~0.4 m, assumed it was passing, and flew blind into the noodle. Two causes, both
  fixed above: it aimed ~23 cm high (camera pitch) and came in off-axis.

  Ablation at level 1, 100 episodes:
    no climbing during search          0.65 pass/min  15% crashed
    with climbing                      0.82 pass/min  19% crashed
    fast strafe flip (no smoothing)    0.76 pass/min  11% crashed   (sim has no detector noise)
  The fast flip looks best in the sim but wobbled on the real drone (rc lr flipping +-40
  every tick), because real ratio jitter is ~0.02-0.17 per frame. The sim needs detector
  noise before it can tell these apart.

  Wall contact by phase (steps on the fence over 100 episodes):
    approach / lining up 4216, go-round sidestep 175, go-round reverse 106,
    go-round return 25, search 21.
  First touch: lining up 85 episodes, go-round 8, search 1.
  First-touch action: sideways strafe 73, up/down 10, forward 2.

IMPLICATIONS FOR THE MDP (only reward and GAMMA are in the autopilot's control)
  - R_WALL 0.5 a step is cheap next to R_PASS 40: an agent can lean on the fence for 80
    steps for the price of one pass. Real flights cannot afford one wall step. Consider a
    large first-contact cost, or one that grows with consecutive wall steps.
  - The obs already carry what lining up needs (ratio, sin_2t, cos_2t). A small
    potential-based term on roundness while visible (phi += k * ratio * visible) would pay
    for lining up without being farmable, since it telescopes. Do not pay ratio unless the
    marker is seen, or it also pays for lining up with the back.
  - Do not shape on v -> 0 (camera pitch, point 4). If anything, v -> -0.58.
  - Searching upwards matters. A reward that charges for the hoop being out of view can
    push the agent to hover where it last saw something; the progress term should dominate.
  - Timeouts dominate the baseline. If a run's passes/min stalls with low crashes, check
    the timeout share: the agent is likely failing to get round, not crashing.
